## Overview

My PhD research focuses on **Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) applied to reactive flows**, with particular emphasis on the **modeling of turbulent hydrogen–air diffusion flames**. The project combines a detailed physical characterization of these flames with the development and assessment of a numerical modeling approach.

**Institution:** [MEGEP / CERFACS]  
**Period:** 2022 – 2026 
**Supervisors:** Poinsot Thierry - Bertier Nicolase
**Thesis title:** Theoretical analysis of diffusion flames and development of a Large Eddy Simulation model for turbulent hydrogen diffusion flames

## Scientific context and motivation

Hydrogen combustion raises important challenges for the numerical description of turbulent reacting flows. In this thesis, the focus is on **non-premixed (diffusion) flames**, where fuel and oxidizer are initially separated and mixing strongly influences the combustion process.

## Research objectives

The main objective is to **develop a model for turbulent hydrogen–air diffusion flames** supported by a thorough physical characterization of the underlying flame behavior.

More specifically, this project aims to:

1. **Characterize** relevant physical features of hydrogen–air diffusion flames.
2. **Identify** the processes and quantities that matter for numerical modeling in turbulent conditions.
3. **Develop and assess** a modeling approach for the targeted flame configurations.
